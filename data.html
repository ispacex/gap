The reproducers of findings 2–9 are in the text of Findings and in the tests of their pull requests. Each test fails on master and passes with the fix; finding 21 has no test, since after #96 its input no longer reaches the code.

Branches: each fix is a branch of ispacex/anupq (the head of its pull request), and correctness at b7deefa is master 81bcf35 with all fixes #96–#121 merged and the union-find orbit speed-up (ispacex/anupq#2, same output); 123 test files pass on it.

This site is built from src/ on its own branch (gh-pages of ispacex/gap): python3 src/build.py writes the English pages and the Russian ones under ru/ from one set of numbers and tables.
