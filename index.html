ANUPQ: a correctness audit

We use ANUPQ (PqDescendants, PqPCover, PqStandardPresentation) to walk the 2-group generation tree to order $2^{11}$, where a missed or a spurious descendant would silently change a mathematical result. The maintainers have said that they care for correct computations; so do we. This is what we checked, what we found, and the fixes we propose, each with a reproducer, an argument for why the fix is right, and the evidence.

In short. Nine defects with a reproducer each, fixed in nine small pull requests: two give wrong descendants silently (one of them also crashes pq), one gives a wrong isomorphism, one more crashes pq, one hangs, and four are errors or wrong data on less used paths. Against the SmallGroups library, PqDescendants on the library's own groups is right everywhere we looked (3296 pairs (group, step size), $p = 2, 3, 5, 7, 11$), and standard presentations are invariant and separating on 927 groups. The two silent errors are reached only off that path: a pc presentation other than pq's own, or 127 or more automorphisms.

How we checked

  1. Reading. The C core (p-quotient and p-cover, collection, consistency, echelon), p-group generation (allowable subgroups, labels, orbits, stabilisers, automorphisms), standard presentations, and the GAP interface, with an eye on boundary cases: $p = 2$ and large $p$, rank 1, class 1, nucleus of rank 0, step size larger than the nucleus, many generators, many automorphisms, interactive and non-interactive use.
  2. Differential checks against independent data (details): descendants against the SmallGroups library, the same group in other pc presentations, standard presentations as an isomorphism invariant, pq under AddressSanitizer.
  3. Every fix comes with a test that fails before and passes after, and the package test suite passes with each fix and with all of them together (107 test files, 0 failures).

Found correct

  • PqDescendants on SmallGroups: for every group $G$ of order $p^m$ and every step size $s$ with $p^{m+s}$ in range, the descendants are exactly the groups $H$ of order $p^{m+s}$ with $H/P_c(H) \cong G$ (as multisets of IdGroup): $p = 2$ to order $2^8$, $p = 3$ to $3^6$, $p = 5$ to $5^5$, $p = 7, 11$ to $p^4$; 3296 pairs, 0 differences.
  • PqStandardPresentation as an isomorphism key: all groups of orders 2, 3, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 243, 625, 729 get pairwise different standard presentations, each is a presentation of its group, and isomorphic copies in other pc presentations get the same one (927 groups, 0 failures).
  • The collectors for $p = 2$ and odd $p$, consistency and tails, echelonisation, label/subgroup conversion, the allowability test $U + N = M$, the central automorphisms, the insoluble orbit algorithm, and the permutation representation of $A/S$ handed to AutPGrp (PR #85): read and found right.

Environment

GAP 4.16.1, AutPGrp 1.13.0, ANUPQ master 81bcf35 (with GMP), macOS 26 on arm64 (Apple clang); the checks of the fixes were repeated with pq built with -fsanitize=address. Everything needed to repeat them is on Data and scripts.

An audit of the GAP package ANUPQ (gap-packages/anupq, master at 81bcf35) by the D₅ project (ispacex/dimension-subrings), which relies on ANUPQ for p-group generation. As of 18 October 2026.
