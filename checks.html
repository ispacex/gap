Differential checks

Descendants against the SmallGroups library

For a $p$-group $H$ of $p$-class $c \ge 2$, its parent in the tree is $H / P_c(H)$ and its step is $\log_p |P_c(H)|$. So for $G$ of order $p^m$ and a step $s$ the immediate descendants of $G$ are exactly the groups $H$ of order $p^{m+s}$ with $H / P_c(H) \cong G$. desc_vs_sg.g compares, for every $G$ in the library and every $s$ in range, the multiset of IdGroup of PqDescendants(G : StepSize := s) with that set (elementary abelian groups of rank $\ge 5$ only for $s = 1$: larger steps there take hours).

Descendants of other presentations of the same group

The descendants of a group cannot depend on its presentation. coords.g compares PqDescendants(G : StepSize := 1) for $G$ = SmallGroup(n, k) ($n$ = 32, 64, 128, $p$-class $\ge 3$, every third $k$) with three isomorphic copies: the special pcgs, a pcgs of a permutation representation, and pq's own presentation of the fp group. On master, in this run, the first difference appears at order 128 (SmallGroup(128, 3): 10 descendants, 8 for a copy), and 17 groups differ before pq dies at SmallGroup(128, 1728); with fix 1: 1983 (in each of four runs) comparisons, 0 differences, also under AddressSanitizer with three seeds. The fixed list of copies in the table of finding 1 (gen_codes.g, check_codes.g) is the reproducible version of this check.

The pq2048 walk with its own covers and children

With PQ2048_OWNCOVER=1 PQ2048_OWNDESC=1, the D₅ project builds $p$-covers with GAP's Extension and step-1 children as pc quotients of them, and calls PqDescendants on those children: exactly the inputs of finding 1. For all 304 vertices of orders 16–64 of class $\le 3$, the 1186 children were compared with their SmallGroups copies (descendants by IdGroup, up to order 256): 0 differences on master. Beyond that we did not check; the fix removes the question.

AddressSanitizer

pq built with -fsanitize=address, run with the fixes through the copies above and the standard presentation check of order 64: no report. On master, the crash of finding 1 is a heap-buffer-overflow in stabiliser_generators (stabiliser.c:173): with maps that are not automorphisms a Schreier word never closes, and the loop writes past the end of the workspace, which it never checks (see Open items). The crash of finding 4 leaves no report: pq exits on the input that does not fit it.

An audit of the GAP package ANUPQ (gap-packages/anupq, master at 81bcf35) by the D₅ project (ispacex/dimension-subrings), which relies on ANUPQ for p-group generation. As of 18 October 2026.
