ANUPQ: a correctness audit

We use ANUPQ (PqDescendants, PqPCover, PqStandardPresentation) to walk the 2-group generation tree to order $2^{11}$, where a missed or a spurious descendant would silently change a mathematical result. The maintainers have said that they care for correct computations; so do we. This is what we checked, what we found, and the fixes we propose, each with a reproducer, an argument for why the fix is right, and the evidence.

In short. 24 defects, each with a reproducer, all fixed in pull requests #96–#121.
  • First pass (#96–#104, nine defects): two give wrong descendants silently (one of them also crashes pq), one gives a wrong isomorphism, one more crashes pq, one hangs, and four are errors or wrong data on less used paths. The two silent errors are reached only off the default path: a pc presentation other than pq's own, or 127 or more automorphisms.
  • Second pass (#105–#121, fifteen defects): we checked each of the 15 suspected defects that were left open: thirteen are confirmed and two refuted. Three more turned up on the way; two are fixed here, the third is harmless and left open. Ten of the fifteen can give a wrong result without any message, but none of them is reached by 2-group generation with default options: they need the option PcgsAutomorphisms, automorphisms supplied by hand together with an exponent law, a prime $p \ge 2^{15}$ or $p^d > 10000$, a pc group that is not a $p$-group, a pQuotient of class 2 or more in another pc presentation, more than 65535 pc generators, or a workspace near its limit.
Against the SmallGroups library, PqDescendants on the library's own groups is right everywhere we looked ({{sg_pairs}} pairs (group, step size), $p = 2, 3, 5, 7, 11$), and standard presentations are invariant and separating on {{sp_groups}} groups.

How we checked

  1. Reading. The C core (p-quotient and p-cover, collection, consistency, echelon), p-group generation (allowable subgroups, labels, orbits, stabilisers, automorphisms), standard presentations, and the GAP interface, with an eye on boundary cases: $p = 2$ and large $p$, rank 1, class 1, nucleus of rank 0, step size larger than the nucleus, many generators, many automorphisms, interactive and non-interactive use.
  2. Differential checks against independent data (details): descendants against the SmallGroups library, the same group in other pc presentations, standard presentations as an isomorphism invariant, pq under AddressSanitizer.
  3. Every suspicion read in the code was then reproduced or refuted on master and on the branch with the earlier fixes; for each confirmed one we state whether it can give a wrong result without a message and whether our own runs can reach it.
  4. Every fix comes with a test that fails before and passes after (except where the input no longer reaches the code after an earlier fix, as for finding 21), and the package test suite passes with each fix and with all of them together.

Found correct

  • PqDescendants on SmallGroups: for every group $G$ of order $p^m$ and every step size $s$ with $p^{m+s}$ in range, the descendants are exactly the groups $H$ of order $p^{m+s}$ with $H/P_c(H) \cong G$ (as multisets of IdGroup): $p = 2$ to order $2^8$, $p = 3$ to $3^6$, $p = 5$ to $5^5$, $p = 7, 11$ to $p^4$; {{sg_pairs}} pairs, 0 differences.
  • PqStandardPresentation as an isomorphism key: all groups of orders {{sp_orders}} get pairwise different standard presentations, each is a presentation of its group, and isomorphic copies in other pc presentations get the same one ({{sp_groups}} groups, 0 failures; order 128 is being rerun).
  • The collectors for $p = 2$ and odd $p$, consistency and tails, echelonisation, label/subgroup conversion, the allowability test $U + N = M$, the central automorphisms, the insoluble orbit algorithm, and the permutation representation of $A/S$ handed to AutPGrp (PR #85): read and found right.

Environment

GAP 4.16.1, AutPGrp 1.13.0, ANUPQ master {{master}} (with GMP), macOS 26 on arm64 (Apple clang); the checks of the fixes were repeated with pq built with -fsanitize=address. Everything needed to repeat them is on Data and scripts.
