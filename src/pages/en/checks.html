Descendants against the SmallGroups library

For a $p$-group $H$ of $p$-class $c \ge 2$, its parent in the tree is $H / P_c(H)$ and its step is $\log_p |P_c(H)|$. So for $G$ of order $p^m$ and a step $s$ the immediate descendants of $G$ are exactly the groups $H$ of order $p^{m+s}$ with $H / P_c(H) \cong G$. desc_vs_sg.g compares, for every $G$ in the library and every $s$ in range, the multiset of IdGroup of PqDescendants(G : StepSize := s) with that set (elementary abelian groups of rank $\ge 5$ only for $s = 1$: larger steps there take hours).

Descendants of other presentations of the same group

The descendants of a group cannot depend on its presentation. coords.g compares PqDescendants(G : StepSize := 1) for $G$ = SmallGroup(n, k) ($n$ = 32, 64, 128, $p$-class $\ge 3$, every third $k$) with three isomorphic copies: the special pcgs, a pcgs of a permutation representation, and pq's own presentation of the fp group. On master, in this run, the first difference appears at order 128 (SmallGroup(128, 3): 10 descendants, 8 for a copy), and 17 groups differ before pq dies at SmallGroup(128, 1728); with fix 1: {{coords_total}} comparisons in each of four runs, 0 differences, also under AddressSanitizer with three seeds. The fixed list of copies in the table of finding 1 (gen_codes.g, check_codes.g) is the reproducible version of this check.

The pq2048 walk with its own covers and children
